# QGAIN v4.0.0 Reviewed Scientific Contract — Candidate

## Decision

Retain four measurements, preserve their public feature names for compatibility,
and change the family-level interpretation from **gain dynamics** to **recorded
level and level dynamics**. Permanently exclude the v3.0 local-transition/step
detector from the reviewed production module.

This candidate is not a publication freeze. It becomes freeze-eligible only
after the local cohort run, migration audit, G1–G10 review, and explicit G10
feature decisions.

## Why the family remains valuable

The four estimators make the amplitude operating context of a remote recording
observable. They can expose low/high operating levels, short-term level spread,
segment-level shifts, and ordered drift. These quantities are directly relevant
to the interpretation and reliability of amplitude-sensitive acoustic features.

The measurements are not acquisition-specific. The observed amplitude envelope
contains both speaker physiology/behavior and acquisition scaling. No-reference
audio cannot determine whether the same dB trajectory was generated by device
gain, microphone distance, vocal effort, respiration, fatigue, or dysarthria.
The reviewed notebook therefore treats source non-identifiability as a required
G5 result rather than pretending to eliminate it.

## Retained features

### `qgain_typical_speech_level_dbfs`

Contextual median AC-RMS level in guarded strict speech. It is analysis-view
dBFS, not calibrated SPL and not ITU-T P.56 active speech level. It is
non-ordinal and cannot independently reject a recording.

### `qgain_within_segment_iqr_db`

IQR of frame levels after subtraction of each usable segment median. It measures
short-term recorded-level dispersion after removing segment offsets. It is
sensitive to gain modulation and equally sensitive to physiological/prosodic
amplitude modulation.

### `qgain_between_segment_mad_db`

Normal-consistent MAD scale of segment-median levels. The exact multiplier is
stored as a parameter. The unscaled MAD is exported as an audit companion. The
result is a robust dispersion index, not an unbiased standard deviation and not
a pure gain estimate.

### `qgain_abs_drift_db_per_min`

Absolute Theil-Sen slope of segment-median level against original time. The
signed slope, confidence interval, pair count, span, and time-order permutation
audit are retained. This is the highest phenotype-confounding-risk feature.

## Blocking corrections from v3.1

1. Correct the signal-view label. The existing decoder creates a mono,
   global-DC-removed, resampled 16-kHz analysis view; it is not “DC preserved.”
2. Correct computational-floor classification so nonzero sub-floor frames are
   marked as floor affected instead of merely clamped.
3. Use only usable segments in the pooled within-segment IQR.
4. Make the 1.482602... MAD multiplier explicit and export unscaled MAD.
5. Add segment-balanced audit companions for pooled-vs-segment weighting.
6. Add polarity, time-shift, DC-offset, and preprocessing-provenance tests.
7. Add the required source non-identifiability demonstration and time-order
   permutation audit.
8. Standardize long-form and ML-facing exports with masks, status, and support.
9. Prohibit standalone QGAIN reject/accept thresholds until biomarker-specific
   calibration exists.

## G1–G10 contract

- **G1:** Exact frozen segmentation, decoder, downmix, resampling, normalization,
  and parameter/code hashes. Amplitude-normalized or untracked DRC inputs fail.
- **G2:** Hand-computable AC-RMS, median, IQR, MAD, and Theil-Sen tests; missing
  remains missing.
- **G3:** Typical level is gain-equivariant; dynamics are gain-invariant. All
  features are polarity-invariant and invariant to a common signal/interval
  time shift. Resampling/codec sensitivity is quantified.
- **G4:** Independent dose grids for amplitude modulation, segment offsets, and
  linear dB ramps.
- **G5:** Constant-RMS spectral-change controls plus an explicit demonstration
  that a speaker-intensity envelope and a device-gain envelope are
  observationally identical. The gate passes only with bounded claims.
- **G6:** Guard, floor threshold, segment-frame minimum, segment weighting,
  segment deletion, count, duration, and span sensitivity.
- **G7:** Availability, distributions, outliers, boundary piles, support tiers,
  participant/task clustering, and signal-linked examples.
- **G8:** Determinism, bootstrap/delete-one precision, feature redundancy,
  repeated-recording persistence reported as empirical persistence—not proof of
  estimator reliability—and comparison against a pinned official ITU-T P.56
  implementation. The P.56 comparator is still open in this Stage 1 candidate
  and therefore blocks publication freeze.
- **G9:** Not applicable because the rejected step detector is absent.
- **G10:** Per-feature `retain`, `retain_contextual`, `retain_with_high_confounding`,
  `revise`, or `drop`; no family-level acceptance can hide a failed feature.

## Open pre-freeze item

The Master Feature Design requires comparison with, but not relabeling as,
ITU-T P.56 active speech level. Stage 1 intentionally does not reimplement that
standard from memory. Before QGAIN can freeze, integrate and pin the official
G.191 Speech Voltmeter implementation, validate the executable against its
reference tests, and report its agreement/difference from the contextual median
AC-RMS feature.

## ML deployment contract

QGAIN values may enter a multimodal pipeline only together with availability,
status, support tier, measurement version, and signal-view identity. The
extractor performs no imputation and defines no “reliable/unreliable” threshold.
Future studies must calibrate biomarker-specific reliability or abstention
functions, for example

`P(biomarker b is within an acceptable error | QGAIN, other Q families, support, task)`.

A model may use QGAIN as context, uncertainty input, or a trigger for an
alternative extractor only after that calibration. It must not treat higher
QGAIN values as universally worse.
